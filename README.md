# vector-racer
Play an inertia-driven car racing video game. It simulates physics, acceleration, and momentum in a very simple fashion that the players can understand. Players have full control over their speed but must manage braking carefully.

You can play solo or race up to one other person. 

Don't crash into the walls or the other players! Use WASD to change where you want to go. Press enter after every command, and press enter to confirm movement.

You may slipstream if you are exactly one space behind a player and that is the main direction you are moving in. Slipstreaming means you move one extra space in the main direction you are going in. If you don't plan it carefully, this could mean you crash into another player while slipstreaming!
